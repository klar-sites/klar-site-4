Our origin

Velocity began as a frustration — then became a platform.

Before Velocity, our founders led product and engineering teams at fast-growing SaaS companies. The pattern was always the same: talented teams, big ambitions, and a stack of tools that made every release feel harder than it should.

We imagined a workspace where strategy, execution, analytics, and deployment could live together — where teams could move quickly without losing visibility or quality.

Today, Velocity helps modern product teams turn ideas into shipped improvements with fewer handoffs, cleaner workflows, and real-time insight into what’s working.

“

Great teams don’t need more tools. They need one shared system that makes every decision, handoff, and launch feel lighter.

Maya Ellis, Co-founder & CEO

Company timeline

From prototype to global platform

We’re still early in our journey, but every chapter has been shaped by the teams who trusted us with their product velocity.

2019

The first prototype

Velocity started as an internal deployment and analytics layer for one product team moving at startup speed.

2021

Public beta launch

After 18 months of private pilots, we opened Velocity to early customers and saw teams cut release cycles in half.

2023

Enterprise-ready scale

We launched SSO, audit logs, granular permissions, and a global edge network for mission-critical product teams.

2025

The next era of product velocity

Today we’re building AI-assisted workflows that help teams prioritize, ship, measure, and learn continuously.
